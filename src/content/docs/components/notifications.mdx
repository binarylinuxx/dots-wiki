---
title: Notifications and sidebar
description: Notification popups, the sidebar with their history, quick tiles and the volume slider
---

blxshell is its own notification daemon, unless another one is already
running when it starts.

## Popups

New notifications appear at the top right (or left) and stack, newest on top.
Pointing at the stack spreads it out. **Settings → Panels → Notifications**
sets the side, the width, how many show at once, and whether images show.

## Sidebar

The sidebar holds the notification history. Pull it out of the **top-right
corner**: press there and drag left; when you let go it finishes opening or
closing, whichever is nearer. On zwwm, a touch swipe in from the right edge does the same.

Under it is the **utility stack**, pulled out of the **bottom-right corner** on
its own:

- quick tiles for Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, the microphone, do not disturb, Quick
  Share, the settings app and the lock screen (each can be hidden);
- a media card for the active player;
- screen brightness, keep awake, and the screen recorder (full screen or a
  region, with or without sound).

From a bind:

```sh
blxshell sidebar             # both parts
blxshell sidebar top         # the notifications only
blxshell sidebar bottom      # the utility stack, sliding in
blxshell sidebar corner      # the utility stack, growing out of the corner
```

**Settings → Sidebar** sets its width, the tiles' size and corners, the cards
and the size of the corner you pull from.

## Volume

A slider for output and input volume comes out of the right edge, halfway down:
tug it out with the pointer, or `blxshell osd`. It can also show itself
whenever the volume changes (**Settings → Panels → Volume**).
