<section class="home-parts">
	<h2>What's in it</h2>
	<dl>
		<div><dt><a href="/components/bar/">Bar</a></dt><dd>Workspaces, window title, tray, clock and status, with plugin modules where you want them.</dd></div>
		<div><dt><a href="/components/launcher/">Launcher</a></dt><dd>Apps, favourites and usage ranking; doubles as the wallpaper picker.</dd></div>
		<div><dt><a href="/components/notifications/">Notifications</a></dt><dd>Popups and a sidebar history, pulled out of the screen corner.</dd></div>
		<div><dt><a href="/components/settings/">Settings</a></dt><dd>A separate app that edits the config; the shell follows every change live.</dd></div>
		<div><dt><a href="/components/wallpapers/">Wallpapers</a></dt><dd>Colours generated from the wallpaper with matugen, or fixed themes.</dd></div>
		<div><dt><a href="/components/greeter/">Login screen</a></dt><dd>A greetd greeter in the lock screen's style, with your colours.</dd></div>
	</dl>

<section class="home-facts">
	<div><strong>One surface</strong><span>Frame, bar and panels are a single signed-distance shape, so panels melt out of the edges instead of sliding over them.</span></div>
	<div><strong>Plugins</strong><span>Bar modules and desktop icons in any language, over JSON lines. <a href="/plugins/discover/">Browse them</a>.</span></div>
	<div><strong>Small</strong><span>About 16,700 lines of C++ and GLSL, on <a href="https://github.com/binarylinuxx/vw">vw</a>.</span></div>
</section>
